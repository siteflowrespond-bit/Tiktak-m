    How do I turn this on in OBS?
    1. Open OBS Studio (version 28 or newer).
    2. Go to Tools → WebSocket Server Settings.
    3. Tick Enable WebSocket server. Leave the port at 4455.
    4. Click Show Connect Info and copy the password into the box above.
    5. OBS on another computer? Use its address, for example ws://192.168.1.20:4455.
