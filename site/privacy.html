Privacy

Privacy statement

Last updated 25 November 2026. Applies to the flock.tt desktop app and the website flocktt.xyz.

The short version

  • flock.tt runs entirely on your own computer. There are no flock.tt servers, accounts or logins.
  • We don't collect, sell or share any personal data. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no telemetry.
  • The only things the app sends over the internet are what's needed to connect to TikTok LIVE and show your stream's pictures, listed below.
  • Your settings, passwords and uploaded sounds are stored only on your computer, and you can delete them at any time.

What the app stores, and where

Everything is saved in one folder on your computer and never uploaded:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\flock.tt
StoredWhy
Your TikTok usernameSo the app can connect to your LIVE.
Your rules, goals, layouts and settingsSo your setup is there next time.
OBS WebSocket password (optional)To control OBS on your computer. It is never shown to the browser or the overlays.
Euler Stream API key (optional)Only if you add one in Settings, to raise TikTok connection limits.
Sounds you uploadTo play them on stream.
TikTok's gift listGift names, coin values and links to TikTok's gift pictures, so gifts show the right picture.
Small display preferencesFor example which page was open last. Kept in the app's own storage.

Uninstalling flock.tt and deleting that folder removes all of it.

What the app sends over the internet

  • TikTok. When you press Connect, the app connects to TikTok LIVE for the username you typed, to receive that stream's public events: chat, gifts, likes, follows, shares and joins. This is the same public information anyone watching the LIVE can see. It uses the open-source tiktok-live-connector library. flock.tt is not made by or affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.
  • Euler Stream. To connect, TikTok requires the request to be signed. The library sends the connection request, including the TikTok username, to the Euler Stream signing service (eulerstream.com), plus your API key if you added one. Their own privacy policy applies to that service.
  • TikTok's picture servers. Overlays load viewers' profile pictures and gift pictures straight from TikTok's image servers, the same way the TikTok app does.
  • OBS. If you connect OBS, the app talks to OBS on your own computer (or the address you enter). This normally never leaves your computer or local network.
  • Google Fonts. The app's screens load the Geist font from Google Fonts, which means Google sees your IP address when the font is downloaded.

The app does not check for updates in the background, report crashes, or send any usage information to anyone.

Your viewers

While you're live, flock.tt shows viewers' public TikTok names, profile pictures and messages on your screen and overlays, and can read chat aloud using your computer's built-in voices. This is kept in memory while the app is running and is not saved to disk or sent anywhere.

Security

  • The app's local server listens only on 127.0.0.1, so other devices can't reach it unless you change that on purpose.
  • Other websites open in your browser are blocked from controlling the app.
  • Links in the app only open normal web pages, and the app refuses camera, microphone and location requests.

The download

  • The installer (flocktt-setup.exe) is built automatically by GitHub Actions from the app's source code and published on GitHub Releases. The Download button on this site links there.
  • Each release lists the installer's SHA-256 fingerprint, so you can check your download wasn't changed.
  • The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click More info then Run anyway.
  • The installer doesn't add toolbars, other software, background services or start-up items.

This website

  • flocktt.xyz is a plain static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or ads.
  • GitHub, as the host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The site loads the Geist font from Google Fonts.
  • The demo runs entirely in your browser with made-up viewers. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

Children

flock.tt doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. TikTok LIVE has its own age rules.
