The short version

  • flock.tt runs on your own computer. There are no flock.tt accounts or logins, and your settings, sounds and records never leave your computer.
  • The only thing flock.tt can ever share about you is your public TikTok @username, on the public streamer list, and only if you say yes. It asks you once, and you can change your mind any time.
  • We don't sell data, and there's no analytics, tracking, advertising or cookies. Nothing about your viewers is ever collected.
  • Otherwise the app only goes online to do what you ask: connect to TikTok LIVE, show your stream's pictures, talk to OBS, find sounds, and download updates.
  • Your settings, passwords and sounds are saved only on your computer. You can delete them at any time.
  • TikTok's own rules still apply to you: you must be 18 or older to go LIVE and to send or receive gifts. See Age and TikTok rules.

Who we are

flock.tt is an independent, free tool for TikTok LIVE creators. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance. "TikTok" and "TikTok LIVE" are trademarks of their owners and are used here only to describe what the app works with.

Because flock.tt has no accounts and your settings stay on your computer, we are not able to see, access or recover anything you do in the app. The one exception is the opt-in streamer list.

The streamer list

flock.tt keeps a public list of creators who use it, showing a red dot next to anyone who's live right now. You're only on it if you say yes.

  • You're asked once, the first time you connect to a LIVE. Nothing is sent before you answer, and nothing is sent if you say no.
  • What's on it: your TikTok @username (which is already public while you're live), the flock.tt version, Windows or Mac, how many LIVEs you've done with flock.tt, and when you were first and last seen. Anyone can see this list.
  • What's never sent: your API key, OBS password, settings, sounds, or anything about your viewers, chat or gifts. Like any website, the list server sees your IP address while it answers, but it doesn't store it.
  • Leaving: in flock.tt, go to Settings and turn off "Public streamer list". You're removed straight away. Your install-key makes sure only your copy of flock.tt (or the flock.tt owner, if you ask) can remove you.
  • Who runs it: the flock.tt owner, on a server they run. The server keeps only what's listed above.

Your viewers

  • While you're live, flock.tt shows viewers' public TikTok names, profile pictures, comments and gifts on your dashboard and overlays.
  • This is kept in memory only while the app is running: the activity feed, top gifters and stats. It is not written to disk and is cleared when you close the app.
  • If you turn on text-to-speech, chat is read aloud with your computer's built-in Windows voices.
  • Anything shown on your overlays is broadcast to your audience as part of your stream, so you are responsible for what you choose to show, under TikTok's Community Guidelines.

What we never do

  • Ask for or store your TikTok password. flock.tt doesn't log in to your TikTok account at all.
  • Post, comment, follow or send gifts on your behalf.
  • Track you, profile you or show ads.
  • Sell or rent data. The only data on our side is the opt-in streamer list, and it's already public.
  • Put you on the streamer list without asking.
  • Install other software, toolbars, background services or start-up items.

Security

  • The app's local server listens only on 127.0.0.1, so other devices can't reach it unless you change that setting on purpose.
  • Other websites open in your browser are blocked from controlling the app. Requests from other sites and from unknown addresses are refused.
  • Your OBS password and API key never leave the app's back end.
  • Links in the app only open normal web pages in your browser, and the app refuses camera, microphone and location requests.

The download

  • The installer, flocktt-setup.exe, is built automatically by GitHub Actions from the app's source code and published on GitHub Releases. The Download button on this site links straight there.
  • The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  • Check your download wasn't changed: each release on GitHub lists the installer's SHA-256 fingerprint. In PowerShell, run Get-FileHash .\flocktt-setup.exe in your Downloads folder and check that the result matches.
  • Only download flock.tt from flocktt.xyz. Copies from anywhere else could have been changed.
  • To uninstall, use Windows Settings → Apps → Installed apps → flock.tt → Uninstall.

This website

  • flocktt.xyz is a plain static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no forms, accounts, cookies, analytics or ads.
  • GitHub, as the host, may log visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the site running.
  • The site loads the Geist font from Google Fonts.
  • The demo runs entirely in your browser with made-up viewers. Nothing you do in it is sent anywhere, and closing the tab clears it.

Age and TikTok rules

flock.tt doesn't collect anyone's age or personal details, but using it with TikTok means TikTok's rules apply to you. In short:

To do this on TikTokMinimum age
Have a TikTok account13 in most countries. 16 in Australia, under the social media minimum age law in force since 10 December 2025.
Host a LIVE (go live)18. Teen accounts (13–17) can watch LIVEs but can't host one. TikTok may ask you to confirm your age.

flock.tt is built for creators who are allowed to go LIVE. Please don't use it to get around TikTok's age rules. Check TikTok's own pages, since they can change:

flock.tt connects through an unofficial, community-made library. TikTok can change how LIVE works at any time, which may stop flock.tt from connecting until it's updated.

Your choices

  • See your data: open %APPDATA%\flock.tt. Everything flock.tt keeps is there.
  • Leave the streamer list: Settings → turn off "Public streamer list". You're taken off it right away.
  • Remove a password or key: close flock.tt and delete config.json in that folder. The app starts fresh next time.
  • Delete everything: uninstall flock.tt, then delete the %APPDATA%\flock.tt folder.
  • Data held by TikTok: your TikTok account data is controlled by TikTok, not flock.tt. Use TikTok's privacy settings or contact TikTok.

Children

flock.tt doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The only thing it can share is a creator's public TikTok @username, with their permission. Under-18s can't host a TikTok LIVE, so flock.tt isn't intended for them.
